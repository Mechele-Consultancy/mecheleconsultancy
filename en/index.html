Frequently asked questions

What business owners ask first

What does it cost?

You receive the prices by email, together with how we work, so you can compare everything side by side. Monitoring is a fixed monthly amount, minimum three months, after which you can stop with a single email. The margin on one tender you win more than covers it.

Why a success fee on top of the price for bid support?

The fixed price covers the work on your bid. You only pay the success fee if you win the contract. If you lose, you pay nothing extra.

I would rather submit myself. Is that possible?

Yes. You receive the matching tenders with a summary and advice, and you choose per tender: submit yourself, or leave the bid support to us.

I already have too much paperwork.

That is exactly why. We look up public data such as your company number and annual accounts ourselves. For each tender, all we ask from you is yes or no.

How many tenders can I expect?

That depends on your sector and region, and we do not promise a number. We only send what really fits your business. The monthly report shows everything that was screened, including in a month without a match.

Is my company big enough?

From about three employees it becomes realistic. If you are smaller, we will tell you honestly and look at most at one concrete tender you can actually deliver.

When do I receive the first tenders?

Monitoring starts on the seventh working day after your first payment. In those days we build your search profile. After that we screen every working day, and a summary follows as soon as there is a tender that fits.

Do you work with companies from outside Belgium?

Yes. Belgian public tenders are open to companies from other EU countries under the same rules. Most tender documents are in Dutch or French; we read them, summarise them for you in English and tell you what is required.
